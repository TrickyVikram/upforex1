Markets Available Through MT5

UPFOREX offers forex, metals, shares, indices and energy CFDs on one MT5 platform. Forex CFDs track currency-pair price moves without physical delivery, reacting to interest rates, inflation and employment data. Gold and silver CFDs follow real metal prices, often driven by inflation, rates and global events, though a CFD gives no physical ownership.

Share CFDs track company prices without stock ownership, while index CFDs group many companies together for broader market exposure. UPFOREX also lists WTI and Brent oil CFDs, which shift with supply, demand, inventory levels and world events. Always check costs, margin and product terms before placing any order.

Forex Trading Regulation and Trust
